Origin / AI disclosure (from the publisher)
-------------------------------------------
Method: AI-assisted (contains AI-generated content).
Base mesh and textures were generated with an AI image-to-3D tool (Meshy),
then cleaned up, scaled, textured and checked in Blender. The skeleton,
skinning, and the walk and run clips were added with Meshy Auto-Rig.

What this game changed
----------------------
The base-color texture was recolored for Don Sterleone: black three-piece
suit, black tie, black shoes, and slicked grey hair and beard. The mesh was
simplified for the web. Idle is derived from the walk clip. Sunglasses, the
white pocket square, the watch chain, and the dress-shoe covers are meshes
parented to the rig in src/game/meshes.ts. Those additions are original to
this repository and may be used with the game.
